# External Trainers: What We Borrowed and Who to Call

Researched November 2026. The shortlist started from the owner's own research. Each provider
was checked online, but most public information is the providers' own marketing or
second-hand summaries, so **verify the details directly before committing**.

## How this fits with Titan Academy
- **Titan Academy is the system that keeps teaching.** It's built on Proximity's own rules
  and October 2026 sessions, so it runs every week whether or not an outside trainer is involved.
- **An external trainer adds live coaching**: fresh eyes, role-play pressure, and a voice the
  team hasn't heard a hundred times.
- **Recommended order:** run the 8-week program first, then bring in an external trainer for
  the six Sales Techs (or the Service Techs) afterwards. Everyone will have a recorded
  baseline (average ticket and close rate), so you can measure whether the outside training
  actually moved the numbers.

## Ideas used in the curriculum (in our own words, credited)
We teach the *principles* below in our own words and credit the source. We have **not**
copied any provider's scripts, workbooks or paid material. If you want the full proprietary
system (for example Sandler or NEPQ), license it from the provider.

| Idea | From | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Find the value gap; people buy peace of mind, not parts; listen more than you talk; diagnose the person, not just the equipment | Joe Crisara / Service MVP (as summarised on ServiceTitan's blog) | Sales x3, x4; Service s2, s7 |
| Check that training actually shows up on real calls, not just in the classroom | Joe Crisara | Sign-offs and deal reviews; ServiceTitan Field Pro if you have it [CONFIRM] |
| Tell the customer "what's going to happen" on arrival; explore their lifestyle and needs before presenting | Nexstar Service System (public overviews) | Field f4; Sales x3 |
| Hands-on skill demonstrations signed off by a supervisor | Nexstar NexTech Academy (public description) | Field sign-offs across all paths |
| The up-front agreement (agree the visit's agenda, including that "no" or "not yet" is OK); qualify pain, budget and decision before presenting | Sandler Selling System | Sales x4, x7, x8 |
| Uncover goals (aspirations) as well as problems (afflictions); show the impact and the "new reality" | RAIN Group (RAIN Selling) | Sales x4, x8 |
| Curious, question-led discovery; consequence questions that let the customer state the cost of waiting | Jeremy Miner / NEPQ | Sales x4, x7 |
| Objection turnaround: pause and acknowledge, clarify, reframe, ask for the next step, one at a time | Jeb Blount, *Objections* (summaries differ on the exact framework names) | Sales x7 |
| Never prejudge, the trust shift, finance three times, anchoring, Scope-Price-Silence, can't vs won't, never discount for free, the two numbers, Dispatch Pro | **Proximity's own October 2026 sessions** | Sales x1–x8; Service s7; Selling e4 |

## The shortlist: my view
| Provider | Best for | What I found | My view |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Joe Crisara / Service MVP** | Technicians who sell on the spot | Still active in 2026: live training, coaching, eLearning and podcasts. His philosophy is to teach technicians to convey value and trust rather than "sell". One third-party site lists about US$5K–15K per rep (unverified). | **First call.** Arguably a better fit for your **Service Techs** (who sell on the spot) than for your six Sales Techs, who already have advanced material. Ask for a demo using a real Proximity blocked drain, hot water and reline estimate. |
| **Nexstar Network** | A whole-company operating system (technicians, office, managers) | Has run training in Sydney and Wollongong (its sewer sales and service system, 2019), Australian plumbing members are reported in 2025, and it publishes a large free video library. | Worth a call if you want an outside system for the office and managers. It's a big commitment and overlaps with what Titan Academy now does for techs. |
| **CertainPath** | Business coaching plus technician sales | Little public detail found on its plumbing sales system or Australian availability. | Compare against Nexstar only if you go the whole-business route. |
| **The New Flat Rate** | Menu pricing | Not researched in depth. | Probably skip. Your pricing lives in the ServiceTitan pricebook, and a second pricing system risks conflicting with "every line from the pricebook". |
| **Sandler** | Commercial relationships and qualifying | Its seven-step Submarine is well documented. Sydney presence not verified. | **Bella and Alex** (commercial), not the techs. |
| **RAIN Group** | Multi-stakeholder consultative sales | RAIN Selling is well documented (*Rainmaking Conversations*). | Second option for Bella and Alex: strata committees and commercial proposals. |
| **Jeb Blount** | Prospecting and outbound pipeline | *Fanatical Prospecting* and *Objections* are widely available. | Bella and Alex building a pipeline of strata and property managers. The books are cheap and a good start. |
| **Jeremy Miner / 7th Level (NEPQ)** | Question-led selling and objection handling | Stage names vary between second-hand summaries. | Useful for questioning, but adapt it carefully: delivered heavy-handed, it can clash with your premium, no-pressure brand. |

### Australian options worth one call (business coaching rather than tech sales specialists)
TradesFormation (Sydney, plumbing, electrical and HVAC), Cube Performance (NSW), Lifestyle
Tradie, UpScale Business Coaching, Profitable Tradie. These are useful for Australian context
(awards, licensing, local market), but none showed a technician-sales program comparable to
Service MVP's.

## Before you sign with anyone
1. Two **Australian plumbing references** with a team of similar size.
2. A **sample session** run by the person who would actually coach your team.
3. A **90-day plan** with weekly practice and manager coaching.
4. **Measurement** of average ticket, close rate, gross profit, cancellations and complaints,
   not just revenue.
5. **Fit with your rules**: no discounting, every line from the pricebook, your option tiers,
   premium positioning. If their method relies on discounts or "specials", walk away.
6. **Rights to the content**: can their material go into Titan Academy, or only be delivered
   by them? (Usually only by them, so get it in writing.)
7. **Train-the-trainer**: can they train you or Harriet (Sales Manager) to keep
   delivering it after they leave?
