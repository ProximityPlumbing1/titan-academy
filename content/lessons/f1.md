## Why we never charge by the hour (but every price is built on hours)

We never charge customers by the hour. They get a fixed upfront price from the pricebook or an approved template, whether the job takes us one hour or three.

That doesn't mean hours don't matter. **Every price is built on hours.** When a job is quoted, someone estimated how long it would take and what materials it would need. The fixed price only works for the business if the work comes in close to that estimate.

So when hours run over, the customer doesn't pay for them. **We do.** Unestimated hours and unbilled materials come straight out of our profit, not the customer's pocket.

That's why the habits in this program matter: being ready at 7am, carrying the right parts, following the standard workflow and avoiding rework. They keep the hours where the price expects them to be.

> **On the job:** A tap repair in Marrickville is quoted as a short job. You forgot the cartridge and have to drive to the supplier. The customer still pays the fixed price. The extra hour is paid for by the business.

## Where every dollar of a job goes, and why discounts hurt

Here's roughly where every dollar of a job goes:

- **About 50%** pays for labour and materials.
- **About 30%** runs the business: vans, the office, dispatch, insurance and everything else that gets you to the door.
- **About 20%** is profit.

On a **$1,000 job, that's about $200 profit**. These are the business's guardrails: 50% gross profit and 20% net profit.

Now look at a discount. Knock 10% off that $1,000 job and you've taken **$100**, which is **half the profit**, for the same work, the same parts and the same hours. That's why we never compete on price. Competitors like Mr Emergency compete on price and speed. We compete on trust and quality.

Techs can't discount or change a price. Only Harriet (Sales Manager) or Sadie (Office Manager) can. Never offer to "ask the office for a better price" in front of a customer: it's a discount by another name.

> **Say it like this:** "I can't change the price, but I can show you the options that fit what you need."
