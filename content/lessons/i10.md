## Leave the site cleaner than you found it and take rubbish away

The last 15 minutes decide the review. Finish every job in the same order: **clean, walk-through, warranty, payment and sign-off, review.** In that order, every time.

Clean comes first because the customer judges the whole job by what they see when you hand it over. A perfect install in a dusty laundry looks like a sloppy job.

- **Leave the site cleaner than you found it.** Wipe down the fixtures and benches you worked on, clean the floor in the work area, and check walls and floors for marks.
- **Take the rubbish away.** Old fittings, offcuts, packaging and the old unit go in the van. Not bagged by the customer's bin, and not left for them to deal with.
- **Lift the drop sheets last**, after the clean, and check underneath them.
- **Put back what you moved**: cupboard contents, bins, furniture.

If you've worked cleanly throughout, this is a quick final pass, not an hour of catching up.

> **On the job:** You finish a hot water job in Kingsgrove ahead of time. The old unit is in the van, but there's a pile of packaging by the side gate and a dusty patch on the laundry floor. The customer is due back from the shops in ten minutes. What gets done before they walk in, and in what order?

## Walk the customer through the finished work and show the before and after

Don't just say "all done". Walk them through it.

- **Show them the finished work** in person: what you did, and how it works.
- **Show the before-and-after photos.** **Customers rarely see what was behind the wall.** The before photo of the corroded pipe or the rusted unit is what makes the after mean something.
- **Show them how to use it**: the new mixer, the isolation valve, the filter cartridge, the hot water controls.
- **Run it in front of them**: the tap, the flush, the shower.
- **Ask if they have any questions**, and answer them properly.

If you found genuine extra work, this is a natural moment to raise it, with photos.

A good walk-through also sets up the happy call. Chloe calls customers after the job, and a customer who understood exactly what was done tells her so.

> **Say it like this:** "Before I go, let me show you what we did. This is what was behind your wall this morning, and this is what's there now."

No job is finished until it's paid and signed off. **Collect the balance and get the customer's sign-off in ServiceTitan before you leave.** No exceptions without office approval.

How it works on installs:

- Planned work is paid **50% upfront** when the plan is approved, and **the balance on completion**. [CONFIRM: whether the 50% rule applies to every planned job] Today, you're collecting the balance.

- **Accounts customers** (property managers, strata, builders) follow their account terms.

## Ask happy customers for a Google review

The last step, and only when the customer is genuinely happy: **ask for a Google review.** Our 4.9 stars from more than 2,500 reviews is the strongest thing the business owns. Every genuine 5-star review earns you **$100** and brings in the next customer.

- **Ask in person, after the walk-through and payment**, while they've just seen the result.
- **Make it easy** for them to do it there and then.
- **Only ask happy customers.** If something isn't right, fix it or call the Install Manager first. A review request on an unhappy job invites a bad review.
- **Keep it genuine.** Never write it for them, and never offer them anything in return.
